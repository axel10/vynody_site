BUILT FOR YOUR LIBRARY

Playback is just the beginning.

From scanning a folder to shaping an album, Vynody makes the small daily tasks of local music feel effortless.

01 / LIBRARY
◫

Your library, your rules

Scan local folders and update your media library incrementally. Browse by song, album, or artist without a recommendation algorithm getting in the way.

↗
02 / LYRICS
〽

Lyrics that keep up

Use LRCLIB for online lyrics with timing and caching. You can also generate, align, and translate lyrics with AI.

↗
03 / DISCOVERY
◎

Identify and complete

Recognize unknown tracks with audio fingerprints, then complete tags and artwork with AcoustID and MusicBrainz.

↗
04 / CONNECTED
⌁

Move freely between devices

Share music files and lyric caches over your local network. Transfer a track or a folder, the way you prefer.

↗
