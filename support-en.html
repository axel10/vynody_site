01 / CONTACT

Contact support

Email zgmf300@outlook.com for help using Vynody, feature requests, or app issues.

Help us help you

Please include your device model, operating-system version, Vynody version, when the issue occurred, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots or a screen recording are helpful. Do not include API keys, access tokens, or other sensitive information.

02 / HELP

Common help

A

Local music is missing

Confirm that Vynody has permission to access music or files in your system settings, then rescan the folder containing your music.

B

Lyrics or metadata cannot be found

Online lyrics, song identification, and metadata lookup require a network connection. Check your connection and make sure the song title and artist are as accurate as possible.

C

Local-network sharing cannot connect

Confirm both devices are on the same trusted local network and that Vynody has local-network permission. Some networks or firewalls block device discovery.
